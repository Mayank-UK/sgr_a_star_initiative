Sahara: - - Description: - Largest hot desert in the world. - Location: - Northern Africa. - Climate: - Arid, extreme temperatures. - Features: - Sand dunes, rocky plateaus (hamadas), gravel plains (regs). - Countries: - Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Mauritania, Sudan, etc. 2. Swahili Coast: - - Description: - Narrow coastal strip along the eastern shore. - Location: - Along the Indian Ocean (Kenya to Mozambique). - Climate: - Tropical, humid. - Features: - Coral reefs, beaches, islands (e.g., Zanzibar). - Significance: - Historic trade region (Arab-African maritime links). 6. Rainforest: - - Description: - Dense tropical rainforest belt. - Location: - Central Africa (especially Congo Basin). - Climate: - Equatorial, hot and humid (Af in Köppen). - Vegetation: - Thick evergreen forests. - Significance: - 2nd largest rainforest after Amazon. 7. African Great Lakes: - - Description: - Series of large freshwater lakes in East Africa. - Notable Lakes: - Victoria, Tanganyika, Malawi. - Features: - Rift Valley lakes, deep basins. - Biodiversity: - High fish diversity (e.g., cichlids). - Importance: - Water resource, fishing, agriculture. 8.
