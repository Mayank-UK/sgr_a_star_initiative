Comprehension


    - Reading comprehension
        - Method
            1. Read the question first and find type of question
                - Most logical and rational meaning/inference
                    - Strictly avoid using general knowledge from outside the passage and stick to the context of the passage
                    - Don't be too strict with language, most of the times, different words are used, look for the context of the statement
                - Meaning/crux of the passage
                    - Answer can be direct from the passage
                    - Don't be too strict with language, most of the times, different words are used, look for the context of the statement
                - Thinking of the author
                    - Keep the passage in mind
                - Assumption from the passage
                    - Very important to not mix own assumptions and general knowledge, strictly algin to the context of the passage
            2. Do a speed reading of passage getting the crux of the passage and finding focus words
            3. Focus on certain words:
                - That shows linkage and causality
                    - Due to, because, but also, by, unless, moreover, etc
                - Degree adverbs (intensity)
                    - Heavily, totally, entirely, too much, etc
            4. Use elimination technique
                - Eliminate extreme statement, 95% if time they are not correct
                - Eliminate the options which shows incomplete data from passage
                - Mark the questions which can be answers, and read passage again keeping the questions in mind
        - Other information
            - Mostly these portions are redundant and can be given less focus:
                - Mathematical figures
                - Elaborations
                - Examples
            - If there are no assumptions then answer can be direct from the passage
            - Some questions are very tricky and there is a high chance of getting them wrong
            - Use hindi language if confused
        - Cheat code of comprehension
            - Traits of mostly correct statements
                - Progressive
                - Logically correct
                - Possibility words - can, could, may, might, etc
                - Focus on broad perspective, wholesome statement, summarized
            - Traits of mostly wrong statements
                - Extreme statements - only, best, none, etc
                - Absurd statement + criticize government
                - Not relevant to passage
                - Very narrow statement - pointing very narrow technique
