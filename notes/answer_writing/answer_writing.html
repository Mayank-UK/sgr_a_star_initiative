Standard frameworks available - - My personal framework (Temp structure) - Introduction - Define the term - Start with a clear, concise, and accurate definition of the core concept or issue in 1–2 sentences. - Use original wording from syllabus, reports, or standard sources where possible. - Link with current affairs, or official data or an interesting fact - Connect the definition to a recent event (within 12–18 months), government data (NITI Aayog, Census, NFHS, SDG Index), or a striking statistic. - Example: "As per the Global Hunger Index 2024, India ranks 105th..." or "The Union Budget 2024–25 allocated ₹X crore to..." - Keep it factual, non-sensational, and directly relevant. - Body - Environmental coverage - How good or bad for environment - Assess ecological footprint: carbon emissions, deforestation, water use, biodiversity loss, pollution levels. - Use lifecycle analysis if applicable (cradle-to-grave impact). - Example and data - Cite specific cases: "Delhi’s odd-even scheme reduced PM2.5 by 13% (CPCB data, 2016)" or "Namami Gange removed 1,000 MLD untreated sewage (2023 report)". - Include both positive and negative impacts with numbers. - Social coverage - Impact on vulnerable groups like women, children, elderly, SC/ST, etc - Analyze differential impact: gender parity, child nutrition, elderly healthcare access, tribal land rights. - Use disaggregated data: "NFHS-5 shows 57% women anaemic vs 25% men". - Impact on community like harmony, education, health, etc - Discuss social cohesion, literacy rates, disease burden, communal relations. - Example: "Ayushman Bharat covered 50 crore beneficiaries, reducing OOP health expenditure by 60% (NHA 2023)". - Impact on family structure - Examine changes in joint vs nuclear families, women’s workforce participation, old-age dependency, child-rearing patterns. - Example: "Maternity Benefit Act amendment increased female LPR by 2.1% in formal sector (PLFS 2023)". - Economic coverage - Impact on growth, employment, inequality, fiscal health - Analyze GDP contribution, job creation (direct/indirect), income distribution, cost-benefit ratio. - Assess impact on public finance, subsidies, revenue leakage, ease of doing business. - Example and data - Cite specific cases: "PLI scheme generated 8 lakh jobs across 14 sectors (DPIIT 2024)" or "GST reduced logistics cost by 15% (Economic Survey 2023–24)". - Include both positive (multiplier effect) and negative (inflation, debt burden) impacts with numbers. - Political coverage - Impact on law/policy/body, politics, etc - Evaluate changes in legislation, institutional reforms, federal relations, electoral politics. - Example: "73rd/74th Amendments empowered 3.2 million women panchayat members (MoPR 2023)". - Impact on federalism and political accountability - Analyze Centre-State coordination, cooperative vs competitive federalism, political polarization. - Example: "One Nation One Election proposal may save ₹10,000 crore per cycle (Law Commission estimate)". - Governance & Administrative coverage - Implementation, monitoring, accountability - Assess delivery mechanisms (DBT, e-governance), institutional capacity, grievance redressal. - Evaluate role of bureaucracy, citizen participation, transparency tools (RTI, social audit). - Example and data - Cite specific cases: "DBT saved ₹2.7 lakh crore in leakages (PMJDY Report 2023)" or "e-Courts disposed 4.5 crore cases digitally (DoJ 2024)". - Include challenges like digital divide, last-mile delivery, corruption index. - Ethical & Human Values coverage - Rights vs duties, probity, integrity, empathy - Analyze conflict between efficiency and equity, public trust, moral dilemmas in policy. - Seshan’s electoral reforms reduced muscle power in 1990s" or "Armstrong Pame built 100-km road via crowdfunding". - Include both ideal (compassionate bureaucracy) and real (conflict of interest, nepotism) scenarios. - Example and data - Cite specific cases: "CoWIN platform enabled 220 crore vaccinations using AI triage" or "Bhashini supports 22 Indian languages in real-time translation (MeitY 2024)". - Include risks like algorithmic bias, digital divide, data colonialism. - Example and data - Cite specific cases: "India–US iCET deepened tech cooperation (2023)" or "India assumed G20 presidency, launched Global Biofuel Alliance (2023)". - Include both opportunities (strategic autonomy) and challenges (border disputes, sanctions). -
