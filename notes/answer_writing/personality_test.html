- Advice from UPSC chairman for personality test - Wear a suit (blue is preferred) - Don't make noise while walking - Greet the chairperson first and then the other members, give proper eye contact to each member while greeting - If they don't ask to sit, ask for their permission to have a seat - Wait for the member to finish speaking and then speak, avoid interrupting the members, the communication should be very fluid - Think and analyse before replying, they should know that your full attention is on listening to them, take good precaution, even in case of not knowing the answer, think before saying that you don't know, they are not judging you on your knowledge right now, but your personality - Answer to the point answers in formal language, don't say "yes", "ya", etc, don't overextend your answers, conclude and stop, let them continue - While answering, 66% eye contact should be given to the questioner, and 33% to the rest of the members
